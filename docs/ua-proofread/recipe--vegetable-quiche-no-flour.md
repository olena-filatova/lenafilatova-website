борошна" (egg and flaxseed FLOUR), which directly contradicts the opening
claim "Без тіста та борошна" (no pastry, no flour) in the very next clause —
the EN deliberately avoids the word "flour" there for this reason, even
though the ingredient itself is flaxseed flour. Changed to "лляного насіння"
to match EN and remove the self-contradiction. `method` step 1 added
"виделкою" (with a fork), a detail not present in the EN ("Whisk eggs
together with flaxseed flour in a large bowl") — removed. Also tightened
step 4 to render "a prepared tin" and "until set and golden on top" (both
previously dropped) rather than just "until golden crust". Everything else
(ingredients, nutrition, imgAlt, title) matched the English and read
naturally — no further changes.
